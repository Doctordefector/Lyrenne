Opening Lyrenne for the first time

Lyrenne is free and open source, and it is not signed with a paid Apple developer
certificate. So the first time you open it, macOS stops it and says it can't check
the developer. You only have to get past this once.

1. Drag Lyrenne onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Lyrenne from Applications. When macOS says it can't verify it, click Done.
3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the line about
   Lyrenne and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older you can skip step 3: Control-click Lyrenne in
Applications, choose Open, then click Open again.

If you prefer Terminal, this does the same thing:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lyrenne.app

Where things go:
  Library, settings and sign-in: ~/Library/Application Support/Lyrenne
  Downloads: ~/Music/Lyrenne (you can change this in Settings)

Updates: when a new version is out, Lyrenne tells you and links the download.
Drag the new Lyrenne into Applications and choose Replace. Your library stays.
