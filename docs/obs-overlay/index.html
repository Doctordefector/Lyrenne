Set It Up

01

In Lyrenne open Settings > Integrations > Lyrenne Link and switch on the stream overlay.

02

Pick a layout (card, lyrics or ticker), a position and a size. The preview shows your current song.

03

Press Copy OBS URL, then in OBS add a Browser Source with that URL at 1920 × 1080.

What You Get

Layouts

Card, lyrics or ticker

A now-playing card with cover and progress, a karaoke block of lyrics, or a slim bar across the top or bottom. Colour from the album art or your own.

Lyrics

Word by word

Lyrics from five providers, filled word by word where the timing exists, with your own edits and timing fixes from Lyrenne carried over.

Files

For Streamer.bot and text sources

Optional now-playing.txt, now-playing.json and cover.jpg, rewritten in a folder you choose on every song change.

Remote

Control it from your phone

Scan a QR code and your phone's browser becomes a remote: play, skip, search, the queue and live lyrics, without leaving the stream. No app to install.

Private

Stays on your PC

The overlay is served from your own machine and only to it. The phone remote is off until you turn it on, and pairs with a private code you can reset.

Gaming

Hotkeys mid-game

Skip a song without leaving the game with Game Mode's global hotkeys, and see what is playing in its in-game overlay.

Questions

Does it work with Streamlabs or other streaming apps?

Anything that can show a web page as a source can use it. For text-only sources, use the now-playing files.

Will my pairing code end up in my OBS scene?

No. The overlay URL carries no code on your own PC, so it is safe in scene collections and screen shares. Only the phone remote needs pairing.

Is it free?

Yes. Lyrenne Music Player is free and open source under GPL-3.0, with no paid tier and no telemetry.
