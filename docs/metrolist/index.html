Move InYour MetroList library, on your PC

Lyrenne Music Player reads MetroList backups, so the library you built on your phone comes with you to Windows, macOS and Linux. Likes, playlists, albums, artists and your play history, in one click.

Download Lyrenne
MetroList 13 & 14 backups · InnerTune family · free · GPL-3.0

Three Steps

01

In MetroList open Settings > Backup and restore and tap Backup. Copy the file to your computer.

02

In Lyrenne open Settings > Backup & Restore > Import from MetroList, or take the step offered during setup.

03

Pick the file, check the preview of what will come over, and press Import.

What Comes Over

Library

Likes, albums, artists

Liked songs, saved albums and followed artists, from MetroList 13 or 14.

Playlists

In the same order

Playlists you made in MetroList keep their song order. YouTube playlists come over as links that open straight from YouTube.

History

Your stats carry on

Play history comes over, so your listening stats and your Rewind recap start where you left off.

Safe

Nothing overwritten

What is already in Lyrenne stays as it is, importing the same backup twice adds nothing, and likes stay local unless you choose to like them on YouTube too.

Coming From Somewhere Else?

Paste a public playlist link from Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, TIDAL or SoundCloud, or paste a list of songs or a CSV export. Lyrenne finds each song on YouTube Music, highlights any match worth a second look, and saves the playlist in Lyrenne or on your YouTube account.
